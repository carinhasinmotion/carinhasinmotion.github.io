About Me

Hi! I'm Bruno Carinhas

I'm a multimedia creative with extensive experience in design, animation, and motion content creation. Throughout my career at Ocubo and in freelance work, I've had the opportunity to work with some of the world's most recognized brands and organizations.

My expertise spans across design direction, 2D and 3D animation, video composition, color grading, visual effects, and creative leadership. I'm passionate about creating visually compelling content that tells stories and resonates with audiences.
